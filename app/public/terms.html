What Contri is

Contri is software that helps people who know each other run a rotating savings circle (ajo, esusu, susu). Money is held and paid out by a smart contract on the NEAR blockchain, through NEAR Intents, not by Contri or any person. The contract's account has no keys, so nobody can change its code. Contri is not a bank, lender or investment product, isn't licensed or regulated, and its team doesn't hold your money.

New, experimental software

Contri was built in November 2026 and is new. It may have bugs. Smart contracts, NEAR, NEAR Intents and the networks you send from can fail or change. Only put in money you can afford to lose, and start with small amounts.

Your responsibilities

  • You must be 18 or older and allowed to use stablecoins where you live.
  • Keep your passkey and your recovery code safe. Passkeys work only on contri.ng, so keep the recovery code in case the site goes away. If you lose both, or clear a browser that kept your key without a passkey, nobody can get your money back. Nobody, including us, can recover your key or move the money in your own accounts for you.
  • Check networks and addresses before you send. Money sent on the wrong network or to the wrong address may be lost.
  • Only join circles with people you trust. A deposit covers one missed payment. If a member misses more, later pots are short, and Contri can't make anyone pay.
  • You are responsible for any taxes on your savings.
  • Don't use Contri for anything illegal, including fraud, money laundering or evading sanctions.

Fees

Contri charges no fee. Network and conversion fees for topping up and cashing out are set by NEAR Intents and the networks you use. The app asks you to send a little more than you need so fees don't leave you short; the extra stays in your account.

Other services your money depends on

Your money is held through NEAR Intents (intents.near), whose operators can pause or upgrade it and lock accounts, and in USDT, which its issuer, Tether, can freeze. The private balance runs on a permissioned network operated by NEAR Intents. 1Click may refund or hold a swap it can't complete. Contri controls none of these.

Blockchain payments can't be undone. Contri can't refund or reverse a payment. Your money isn't insured or covered by any deposit protection scheme.

Contri's website, relayer and keeper may stop working. The contract and NEAR Intents keep your money, and the open-source app and your recovery code can still reach it.
