What Contri is

Contri is software that helps people who know each other run a rotating savings circle (ajo, esusu, susu). Money is held and paid out by a smart contract on the NEAR blockchain, not by Contri or any person. Contri is not a bank, lender, investment product or money transmitter, and doesn't hold your money.

New, experimental software

Contri was built in November 2026 and is new. It may have bugs. Smart contracts, NEAR, NEAR Intents and the networks you send from can fail or change. Only put in money you can afford to lose, and start with small amounts.

Your responsibilities

  • You must be 18 or older and allowed to use stablecoins where you live.
  • Keep your passkey, or your recovery code if you use one, safe. Nobody, including us, can recover your key or move your money for you.
  • Check networks and addresses before you send. Money sent on the wrong network or to the wrong address may be lost.
  • Only join circles with people you trust. A deposit covers a missed payment, but it can't make a member pay every round.
  • Don't use Contri for anything illegal, including fraud, money laundering or evading sanctions.

Fees

Contri charges no fee. Network and conversion fees for topping up and cashing out are set by NEAR Intents and the networks you use, and are shown before you send.
