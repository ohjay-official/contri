Your security is the whole point

Nobody holds the money. Not even us.

In a normal àjọ, one person collects everyone's money and trusts themselves to pass it on. Contri replaces that person with a contract on NEAR: it holds each pot, pays it to whoever's turn it is, and gives deposits back at the end.

  • No collector. The pot pays out in the same moment the last member pays.
  • No password, no seed phrase. You sign in with your face or fingerprint, through a passkey.
  • No names on the chain. Circle names are encrypted on your phone before they are saved.

Every way your circle already saves

Save smartly, together.

Contri works the way àjọ always has, with the parts that used to need trust handled by code.

  • Circles with your people

    Pick the amount, how many people and how often: every 10 minutes to try it, or weekly, or monthly. Share one link on WhatsApp.

  • Turns drawn fairly

    Keep the order people joined, or let the contract draw turns at random once the circle is full, so nobody picks the best turn for themselves.

  • Pay from anywhere

    Send USDT or USDC from your exchange or wallet on BNB Chain, Solana, Tron, TON, Base and more. It arrives as USDT in a couple of minutes.

  • A private balance

    Top up privately and pay any circle from it. The chain sees money arriving from NEAR Intents, never the exchange or wallet behind it.

  • Swap turns

    Need the pot sooner? Buy an earlier turn from someone who can wait. You agree a price and the contract swaps your places.

  • Deposits that cover misses

    Everyone puts down one contribution to join. If someone misses a round, it comes out of their deposit and the pot still pays in full.

How it works

A circle in four steps.

From the first link to the last payout, without anyone holding the money in between.

The full explanation

  1. Start a circle and send the link

    Choose the amount, how many people and how often. Share the invite link on WhatsApp. Only people with the link can join the circle or read its name.

  2. Everyone pays from where their money is

    Each member gets a deposit address for their exchange or wallet. The app shows exactly what to send and follows it until it arrives.

  3. The pot pays out by itself

    When the last person pays, the contract sends the whole pot to whoever's turn it is, in the same transaction. Nobody has to remember to pass it on.

  4. Everyone gets their deposit back

    When the last turn is paid, the contract returns each member's unused deposit. A late round can be settled by anyone, so the circle always finishes.

FAQ

Who holds the money?

No person. Contributions go to a contract on NEAR, a program whose rules anyone can read. It only pays a pot to the member whose turn it is, and only returns deposits to the members who made them. Our team can't move it outside those rules, and its code is public.

What does it cost?

Contri takes no fee. Moving money in and out goes through NEAR Intents' 1Click, whose fees are usually a few cents on BNB Chain or Solana. The app shows what arrives before you send. Contri pays the NEAR transaction fees for every payment you make in a circle.

What if Contri disappears?

The contract keeps running without us, and anyone can settle a round once its deadline has passed. Your money is held in NEAR Intents under keys made from your passkey, and the recovery code from Your key brings them back in the open-source app.

What if I lose my phone?

Sign in on a new phone with the same passkey. Passkeys are synced by Google Password Manager and iCloud Keychain, and every circle account comes back from it. If your passkey is ever deleted, the recovery code you wrote down from Your key brings everything back.

What if a top-up doesn't arrive?

Open Help in the app. It shows every top-up and cash-out made from this phone and where each one is. A swap that can't complete is refunded to your own circle account or private balance, never to anyone else. Still stuck? Copy the details from Help and send them to us.

Is this a bank or an investment?

No. Contri doesn't lend, pay interest or invest your money. It is software that runs a savings circle among people who already trust each other.
