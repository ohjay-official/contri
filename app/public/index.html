Features

Multiple Ways To Save Together.

Save with friends, family or coworkers, from any wallet or exchange, at a pace that suits your circle.

  • Circles With Your People

    Pick the amount, how many people and how often: every 10 minutes to try it, or weekly, or monthly. Share one link on WhatsApp.

  • Turns Drawn Fairly

    Keep the order people joined, or let the contract draw turns at random once the circle is full. The draw uses NEAR's block randomness, so it is best effort: only block producers could sway it.

  • Pay From Anywhere

    Send USDT or USDC from your exchange or wallet on BNB Chain, Solana, Tron, TON, Base and more. It arrives as USDT in a couple of minutes.

  • A Private Balance

    Top up privately and pay any circle from it. The chain sees money arriving from NEAR Intents, never the exchange or wallet behind it.

  • Swap Turns

    Need the pot sooner? Buy an earlier turn from someone who can wait. You agree a price and the contract swaps your places.

  • Deposits That Cover Misses

    Everyone puts down one contribution to join. A missed round comes out of that member's deposit, then out of any backing. Past that, the pot is short.

How it works

Start Saving In 4 Easy Steps.

From the first link to the last payout, without anyone holding the money in between.

The full explanation

  1. Start a circle and send the link

    Choose the amount, how many people and how often. Share the invite link on WhatsApp. Only people with the link can join the circle or read its name.

  2. Everyone pays from where their money is

    Each member gets a deposit address for their exchange or wallet. The app shows exactly what to send and follows it until it arrives.

  3. The pot pays out by itself

    When the last person pays, the contract sends the whole pot to whoever's turn it is, in the same transaction. Nobody has to remember to pass it on, and anyone who paid every round ahead doesn't have to remember to pay either.

  4. Everyone gets their deposit back

    After the last turn is paid, anyone can ask the contract to return each member's unused deposit, and Contri's keeper usually does it within minutes. A late round can be settled by anyone, so the circle always finishes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who holds the money?

No person. Contributions go to a contract on NEAR, a program whose rules anyone can read. It only pays a pot to the member whose turn it is, and only returns deposits to the members who made them. The contract is locked: its account has no keys, so nobody, us included, can change its code or move the money it holds. Anyone can check that on a NEAR explorer.

What does it cost?

Circles are free: paying in, payouts, deposits and trading turns cost nothing, and Contri pays the NEAR transaction fees for every payment you make in a circle. Moving money in from another network or out to one costs a 0.25% fee, shared between Contri and 1Click, which moves it, plus the network's own fee, usually a few cents on BNB Chain or Solana. The app shows what arrives before you send.

What if Contri disappears?

The contract keeps running without us, and anyone can settle a round once its deadline has passed. Your money is held in NEAR Intents under keys made from your passkey. Passkeys only work on contri.ng, so if the site goes away or changes hands, don't use your passkey there: the recovery code from Your key, in the open-source app run with a NEAR account to pay network fees, is how you reach your money. Write it down.

What if I lose my phone?

Sign in on a new phone with the same passkey. Passkeys are synced by Google Password Manager and iCloud Keychain, and every circle account comes back from it. If your passkey is ever deleted, the recovery code from Your key brings everything back, if you wrote it down.

What if someone takes the pot early and stops paying?

Their deposit covers their first missed round, then any backing the circle has covers the rest. Past that, the members paid after them get a smaller pot. A member who still owes when their own turn comes has it held back from their pot and passed to the members who were paid short, so skipping payments doesn't pay off later in the circle. An early receiver who stops paying can still leave others short, and Contri can't make anyone pay, so save with people you trust and give the early turns to the people you trust most.

What if a top-up doesn't arrive?

Open Help in the app. It shows every top-up and cash-out made from this phone and where each one is. A swap that can't complete is refunded to your own circle account or private balance, never to anyone else. Still stuck? Copy the details from Help and send them to us.

Is this a bank or an investment?

No. Contri doesn't lend, pay interest or invest your money. It is software that runs a savings circle among people who already trust each other.
