# Contri: 60-second demo script

Written before any code. If a feature is not in this script, it is not in the first build.

| Time | Voice | Screen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-8s | "In Nigeria we save with ajo. Five friends, 20k naira a week, one person takes the pot. The problem: the person holding the money can disappear." | Founder on camera or voice over a plain title card. |
| 8-16s | "This is Contri. I start a circle: five dollars a day, five friends." | Create circle, share the invite link. |
| 16-26s | "Everyone pays from where their money already is." | One member tops up with USDT on Tron, another straight from an exchange withdrawal. The pot fills. |
| 26-36s | "Nobody holds the pot. When everyone has paid, Contri pays today's person by itself." | The payout lands; show the transaction on the explorer. |
| 36-48s | "Kemi needs cash now. She buys turn one from Dayo, who is happy to wait and earns a dollar for it." | Seat trade: list, buy, order updates. |
| 48-60s | "This week, real circles with real people saved $X on Contri. Ajo, without the collector. Built on NEAR." | Live stats from real circles, then the logo. |

Rules for recording: your own voice, no AI voiceover, under 20 seconds of backstory, no dead time waiting on screen.
